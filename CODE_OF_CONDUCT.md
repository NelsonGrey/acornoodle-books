# Code of Conduct

Acornoodle Books makes books for children and the adults who care for them. We hold our working space to the same standard.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback on work, not on people.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality, including unreleased books and business documents.
- Keep content and discussion appropriate for a children's publisher.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexual or violent content, sharing others' private information, or deliberately undermining others' work.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner, [@mnelson3](https://github.com/mnelson3). The owner will review reports promptly and may remove access or contributions that violate this code.
