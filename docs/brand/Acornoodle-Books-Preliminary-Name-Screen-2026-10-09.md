# Acornoodle Books Preliminary Name Screen

## Decision

Nelson Grey LLC approved **Acornoodle Books** as its working consumer-facing children's publishing imprint on October 9, 2026. The owner elected to proceed without a formal attorney clearance opinion because of current time and budget constraints.

## No-cost checks completed

- Exact web searches for Acornoodle Books and Acornoodle
- Searches combining the name with publisher, books, children's books, trademark, and Amazon
- Preliminary exact-name review of publicly searchable U.S. trademark results
- Basic domain availability check for `acornoodlebooks.com`

## Preliminary findings

- No exact Acornoodle Books publisher, book series, or exact-match U.S. trademark result appeared in the searches completed.
- No obvious children's publishing use surfaced in the exact-name web searches.
- `acornoodlebooks.com` appeared unregistered when checked. It has not been purchased or reserved by this project.
- The name is suggestive of growth, learning, imagination, and play, and it can support books and learning products beyond coloring.

## Important limitation

This is a preliminary, no-cost collision screen, not a comprehensive trademark search, legal opinion, registration, or guarantee that the name is available. Search results and domain status can change, and unregistered or regional rights may not appear in simple searches.

## Risk-reduction controls

- Use the full name **Acornoodle Books** rather than Acornoodle alone.
- Display **An imprint of Nelson Grey LLC** in books and business materials.
- Preserve this dated record of the searches and decision.
- Recheck exact-name, marketplace, trademark, domain, and social-handle results immediately before public launch.
- Do not describe the name as trademark-cleared, legally cleared, or guaranteed unique.
- Consider formal clearance and registration later if sales justify the expense.
