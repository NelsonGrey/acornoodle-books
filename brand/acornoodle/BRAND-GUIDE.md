# Acornoodle Books Brand System

## Brand architecture

- **Corporate identity:** `corporate-logo-primary.png` is the formal Acornoodle Books publisher logo. Use it on business documents, retailer profiles, copyright/title pages, website headers, and publisher-facing materials.
- **Corporate icon:** `corporate-icon-primary.png` is the compact mark-only version for avatars, favicons, small badges, and constrained spaces.
- **Cover identity:** `cover-identity-primary.png` is the energetic child-facing treatment for front covers, back covers, advertising, social graphics, and launch materials.
- **Mascot:** `mascot-reading-primary.png` is the full-color recurring character for the website, marketing, learning prompts, and future merchandise.
- **Interior mascot:** `mascot-reading-line-art.png` is the black-and-white coloring-book version for title matter, activity prompts, completion pages, and other interior appearances.

## Usage rules

1. Keep the corporate logo calm and consistent; do not recolor individual elements to match each book.
2. Use the colorful cover identity only where it remains clearly readable against the background or within a light panel.
3. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or add effects to the supplied assets.
4. Preserve clear space equal to roughly the height of the letter `A` around each logo.
5. Use the line-art mascot in black-and-white interiors so the book remains economical to print.
6. Continue to identify Acornoodle Books as **an imprint of Nelson Grey LLC** in legal and publisher-facing contexts.

## Status

These are approved working production assets derived from the selected concepts. They are raster masters, not formal vector artwork or evidence of trademark clearance.
